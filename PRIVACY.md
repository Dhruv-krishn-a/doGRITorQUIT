# Privacy Policy

gritorquit processes account data, workspace content, and usage telemetry required to provide product functionality, sync, billing, and security.

1. Data Collected
- Account and authentication data
- Workspace content and metadata
- Device and security metadata
- Billing and entitlement state

2. Usage
Data is used to deliver features, ensure security, operate billing, and improve reliability.

3. Retention
Data is retained only as needed for service operation, legal compliance, and fraud prevention.

4. Security
Industry-standard controls are applied for storage, transport, and access control.

5. User Rights
Users may request access, correction, or deletion subject to legal obligations.

6. Contact
For privacy inquiries, contact the gritorquit support team.
